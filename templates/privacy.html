Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and a securely hashed version of your password. When you use the service we store the expenses you enter, including amount, category, date and description.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or any other payment credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to generate your spending summaries and category breakdowns. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and is accessible only through your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorised access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

You can delete individual expenses or your entire account at any time. When you delete your account, your expense data is removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or expense data with third parties. Pages may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can receive basic technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
